Our story...

My name is Anisah and I opened Island Time Cafe in 2026 in Crown Heights, Brooklyn. Born and raised on the beautiful island of Guam, I carry the flavors and spirit of my Chamorro roots with me, even now that I call New York City home.

Island Time Cafe is a heartfelt dream brought to life—a place where I can share the comforting tastes of traditional Chamorro cuisine and bring a little piece of home to my community.

Whether you grew up with these flavors or are discovering Chamorro food for the first time, I welcome you to gather around our table, share in our culture, and experience the love, warmth, and island spirit behind every dish.
